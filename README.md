Emberworks is a round-based tactical FPS on "Foundry Yard", an original dusk-lit foundry map. Vanguard (attack, you + 4 bots) fights Rogue (defence, 4 bots) across three lanes to two bomb sites: plant the bomb or wipe the defenders, then defend the site — or defuse it — as the fuse burns. First to 5 rounds wins. Earn money per round for kills, plants and wins, and spend it in the buy menu on five weapons, armour and frag grenades. Bots push lanes, hold sites, plant, defuse, strafe and react to what they see and hear; headshots, recoil, spread, crouch and armour all matter.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to lock the mouse. WASD to move, Shift to walk, Space to jump, C to crouch, B for the buy menu (buy phase), G to throw a grenade, R to reload, right-click to scope the sniper.
